03 · the mechanism
04 / 10

The rule that does the work

ALLOW eth_sendTransaction WHERE ethereum_transaction.to      == USDC_CONTRACT AND ethereum_calldata.function_name == "transfer" AND ethereum_calldata.transfer._to == 0xPAYEE AND ethereum_transaction.value     == 0

A USDC transfer's transaction.to is the token contract — not the payee. The policy decodes the calldata and constrains the recipient inside it.

Not our error page. The enclave's own words, verbatim:
{
  "error": "RPC request denied due to policy violation",
  "code": "policy_violation"
}

Wrong destination, native-value smuggling, approve, transferFrom, dirty address padding, a batcher wrapper — every attack refused, before a signature could exist.
